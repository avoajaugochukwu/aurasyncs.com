faq:
  - q: "At what age can kids start using affirmations?"
    a: "As soon as they can repeat short phrases — often around age three or four. Keep the words simple and concrete for little ones, and let older kids help pick or write their own."
  - q: "How do I get my child to actually use affirmations?"
    a: "Make it playful and consistent. Turn lines into songs, stick them on the mirror or lunchbox, and say them together at the same time each day, like before school or at bedtime."
  - q: "How many affirmations should my child say a day?"
    a: "Start with one or two. A couple of phrases repeated daily build new thought patterns far better than a long list said once."
  - q: "Do affirmations replace support for a struggling child?"
    a: "No. Affirmations gently encourage self-belief, but they support care rather than replace it. If your child is anxious or low, talk with them and reach out to a teacher or doctor."
