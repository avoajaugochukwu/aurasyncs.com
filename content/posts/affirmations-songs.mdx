faq:
  - q: "What are affirmations songs?"
    a: "Affirmations songs are tracks whose message centers on self-worth, courage, or resilience — empowerment anthems like Roar, Brave, or Three Little Birds. You can pair a song you love with a few present-tense affirmation lines so the feeling and the words reinforce each other."
  - q: "Do affirmations set to music actually work better?"
    a: "There is no proof that singing an affirmation cures anything, but research shows music reliably shifts mood and arousal, and a study by Salimpoor and colleagues found that listening to music we love releases dopamine. Pairing a believable affirmation with a song you enjoy can make the practice easier to keep."
  - q: "How do I build a daily affirmations song ritual?"
    a: "Pick one song per mood — morning, confidence, self-love, resilience — and one or two affirmation lines that feel true for you. Play the song while you get ready, say the lines on the chorus, and let repetition do the slow work over days, not minutes."
