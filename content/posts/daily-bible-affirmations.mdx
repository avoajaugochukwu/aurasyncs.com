faq:
  - q: "What are daily Bible affirmations?"
    a: "Daily Bible affirmations are short, present-tense statements rooted in scripture that you speak over your life each day. Turning a verse into a first-person 'I am' or 'I can' declaration is a long-standing devotional practice — a way to meditate on God's Word and let its truth shape your self-talk."
  - q: "Are affirmations biblical?"
    a: "Speaking and meditating on God's Word is deeply biblical — Joshua 1:8 calls us to keep the Book of the Law on our lips day and night. The caution is in the source: biblical affirmations declare what scripture already says is true, rather than treating our own words as a force that bends reality. Anchor the affirmation to a verse and you stay on solid ground."
  - q: "How do I use daily Bible affirmations throughout the day?"
    a: "Attach them to the rhythm you already have. Speak a morning set as you wake, a midday line when stress builds, an evening one as you wind down, and a night verse as you lie down to sleep. Saying one slowly, like you mean it, matters more than rushing through many."
  - q: "What's the difference between Bible affirmations and regular affirmations?"
    a: "Regular affirmations draw their authority from you — your repetition, your belief. Biblical daily affirmations draw their authority from God's Word; you are agreeing with what scripture already declares, not generating something new. That makes them steadier on the days your own confidence runs thin."
  - q: "Can Bible affirmations replace prayer or church?"
    a: "No. Affirmations are one gentle way to meditate on scripture, but they are not a substitute for prayer, the gathered church, or pastoral care. If you are walking through something heavy, please reach out to your pastor, your community, or a counselor — speaking truth to yourself works best alongside real support."
