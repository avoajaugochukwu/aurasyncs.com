faq:
  - q: "Do sleep affirmations actually help you fall asleep?"
    a: "They won’t knock you out on command, but repeating calm, present-tense lines quiets an overactive mind and shifts focus away from worry, which makes drifting off easier over time. They support good sleep habits; they don’t replace care if insomnia is ongoing."
  - q: "When should I say sleep affirmations?"
    a: "Use them as the last step of your bedtime routine, once you are already in bed with the lights low. You can also reach for them if you wake in the night and your thoughts start to spin."
  - q: "How do I use sleep affirmations effectively?"
    a: "Speak them softly or say them in your mind, and pair each one with a slow exhale. Choose a few that feel believable tonight rather than trying to repeat all of them."
  - q: "What if my mind keeps wandering while I repeat them?"
    a: "That is normal. When you notice the drift, gently return to the line and your breath. The returning is the practice, and over time it gets easier."
