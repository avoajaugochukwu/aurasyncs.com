## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why do affirmations feel so powerful?**

Because they help us speak directly to our subconscious. Words become bridges between how we feel now and who we want to become.

**Q: Can affirmations really shift your mindset?**

Yes. Repeating affirmations creates new patterns in the brain. Over time, you begin to believe what you once only hoped for.

**Q: How should I use these affirmations?**

Try one in the morning, during meditation, or before bed. Speak it slowly. Feel the words—not just say them.

**Q: How can I make my own affirmations?**

Start with a need. Transform it into a present-tense truth. For example, if you seek calm: “I am grounded in peace.” Keep it simple and emotionally resonant.
