faq:
  - q: "What are good Tuesday work affirmations?"
    a: "Tuesday work affirmations are short, present-tense lines that reset your focus after Monday's rush. Try ones like 'I begin today with a clear, steady focus' or 'I am capable of handling whatever this day brings.' Pick two or three and keep them where you'll see them."
  - q: "Why do affirmations help on Wednesday, when the midweek slump hits?"
    a: "Wednesday is the hardest stretch for many people — you're tired from the first half of the week but not yet close enough to Friday to coast. A few honest affirmations won't erase the fatigue, but they can interrupt the 'I can't' loop and remind you that you only have to handle today, not the whole week at once."
  - q: "How should I use these work affirmations during the day?"
    a: "Choose two or three that feel true, say them slowly in the morning, and keep them somewhere visible — a sticky note, your lock screen, the top of your to-do list. Repeat one on a break when your focus dips. Saying it like you mean it matters more than how many times you say it."
  - q: "Do work affirmations actually work?"
    a: "They're a supportive habit, not magic. Research on self-affirmation suggests that reflecting on what matters to you may lower stress and steady your mindset, though the evidence is modest. Affirmations work best alongside good rest, realistic workloads, and real support — not instead of them."
