faq:
  - q: "What makes a sarcastic affirmation different from a regular one?"
    a: "It says the same kind thing — I am capable, I am allowed to rest — but with a smirk instead of a straight face. The joke sneaks the encouragement past the part of you that rolls its eyes at earnest pep talks."
  - q: "Do funny sarcastic affirmations actually do anything?"
    a: "They can help. Humor is considered an adaptive coping strategy, and research has linked humor coping to slightly lower perceived stress, though the evidence is modest and mostly correlational. They are a mood lift and a reframe, not a cure."
  - q: "Are these appropriate for work?"
    a: "Yes. The whole set is PG-13 with no slurs, so you can mutter one before a meeting or stick it on your monitor. The work section is built for inboxes, deadlines, and the meeting that could have been an email."
  - q: "How do I use a short affirmation so it sticks?"
    a: "Pick one or two that make you actually laugh, then say them like you half-mean it. Short is the point — they fit on a sticky note, a lock screen, or under your breath, so you will reach for them when you need them."
  - q: "Can I write my own?"
    a: "Absolutely. Take the thing you are grumbling about, exaggerate it one notch, and land it on something true. I am not lazy, I am energy-efficient is just I am allowed to rest wearing sunglasses."
