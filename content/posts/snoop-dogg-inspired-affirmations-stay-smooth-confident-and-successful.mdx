To make these affirmations work, follow these simple steps:

1. **Speak with confidence** – Say affirmations with belief, just like Snoop does when he drops a verse.
2. **Use present tense** – Act like you already have the success and mindset you want.
3. **Repeat daily** – Affirmations work best when practiced consistently.
4. **Feel the vibe** – Visualize yourself living the life you’re affirming.
5. **Pair with action** – Believe it, then work for it—Snoop didn’t just talk about success; he made it happen.
