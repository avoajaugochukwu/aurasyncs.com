## ❓ FAQ: Using Morning Affirmations

**Why do affirmations stick with people?**

Because they say the thing we feel—but don’t always have words for. They speak directly to the part of us that’s trying to grow, heal, or keep going. That’s why they linger.

**Can affirmations really shift your mindset?**

Yes. When you say something enough, your brain starts to believe it. A few honest words in the morning can shape your choices, your mood, and even your confidence throughout the day.

**How should I use these quotes every morning?**

Pick one. Just one. Say it out loud. Write it down. Tape it to your mirror. You don’t need a big ritual. All it takes is attention and repetition.

**How can I find quotes that feel personal to me?**

Listen for the ones that make you pause, soften, or smile. Keep a journal or notes app just for quotes. Over time, you’ll build your own collection of reminders.
