faq:
  - q: "What are good morning affirmations for men?"
    a: "Good morning affirmations for men are short, present-tense lines that set your tone before the day starts — about discipline, focus, strength, and calm confidence. Pick three or four that feel true, say them aloud, and keep them simple, like 'I do what I said I would do' or 'I meet today with a steady mind.'"
  - q: "When is the best time to say morning affirmations?"
    a: "In the first few minutes after waking, ideally before you reach for your phone. The mind is most open before the day's noise crowds in, so a short set of affirmations then gives your attention a direction to move in rather than reacting to whatever lands in your inbox."
  - q: "How many affirmations should I say in the morning?"
    a: "Three to five is plenty. A short set you actually mean beats a long list you rush through. Saying a line slowly, like you mean it, matters more than how many you stack up. Rotate them as your focus for the season changes."
  - q: "Do morning affirmations actually work for men?"
    a: "They are a supportive practice, not magic. Research on self-affirmation suggests that reflecting on what matters to you can lower stress and reduce defensiveness, though the evidence is modest and works best alongside real action. Said with attention over time, affirmations can steady how you talk to yourself; they pair best with discipline, not instead of it."
