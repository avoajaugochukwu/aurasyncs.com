faq:
  - q: "What are affirmations for kids?"
    a: "Affirmations are short, positive statements a child repeats to shape encouraging thoughts and beliefs. Over time, lines like “I am loved just as I am” help children build confidence and quiet their inner critic."
  - q: "How do I teach my child to use affirmations?"
    a: "Make it a small daily habit. Tie affirmations to your morning or bedtime routine, say them in front of a mirror, or write them on sticky notes. Letting kids invent their own makes the words stick."
  - q: "At what age can children start using affirmations?"
    a: "As soon as they can repeat simple phrases — often around three or four. Keep the words short and concrete for younger kids, and let the affirmations grow with them."
  - q: "Do affirmations really help with a child's anxiety?"
    a: "They can offer comfort and a sense of safety in hard moments, and support emotional regulation. They are a gentle tool, not a replacement for a doctor or therapist if a child is struggling."
