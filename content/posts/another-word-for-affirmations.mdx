faq:
  - q: "What is another word for affirmations?"
    a: "Common synonyms include declaration, assertion, mantra, intention, incantation, and positive self-talk. Each carries a slightly different nuance: a declaration is a bold public statement, a mantra is a short phrase repeated for focus, and an intention is a forward-looking aim. Pick the word that matches the feeling you want."
  - q: "Is a mantra the same as an affirmation?"
    a: "Not quite. A mantra (Sanskrit for 'instrument of thought') is traditionally a short word or sound repeated to steady the mind in meditation, and its power comes partly from the sound itself. An affirmation is a meaningful sentence — usually present tense and first person — repeated to shift your self-talk. In everyday use the words overlap, but a mantra leans shorter and more rhythmic."
  - q: "What is the difference between an affirmation and a declaration?"
    a: "A declaration is a bold, definite statement of what is true or what you stand for, often spoken with conviction or out loud. An affirmation is gentler and more personal — a kind line you repeat to encourage yourself. Many faith traditions use 'declaration' for speaking a verse or promise over your life."
  - q: "Are affirmations and positive self-talk the same thing?"
    a: "They are closely related. Positive self-talk is the broad, everyday habit of speaking to yourself kindly and constructively. Affirmations are one deliberate tool within that habit — specific, prepared lines you repeat on purpose. All affirmations are self-talk, but not all self-talk is a formal affirmation."
  - q: "What is the most formal synonym for affirmation?"
    a: "In formal or written contexts, 'assertion,' 'declaration,' 'avowal,' and 'affirmation' itself are the closest. A thesaurus will also list confirmation, validation, and endorsement — but those mean 'to confirm something is true,' which is different from the self-development sense of a positive statement you repeat."
