faq:
  - q: "What is the heart chakra?"
    a: "The heart chakra, called Anahata in Sanskrit, is the fourth chakra. It sits at the center of the chest and is linked to love, compassion, forgiveness, connection, and self-acceptance. Its element is air and its color is green, sometimes pink. Anahata means unstruck or unhurt — a reminder that something in you stays whole even after heartbreak."
  - q: "How do heart chakra affirmations help?"
    a: "Heart chakra affirmations are short, present-tense statements about love, compassion, and openness. Repeated slowly and with attention, they gently shift the way you talk to yourself about giving and receiving love. They are a supportive practice, not a cure for grief or relationship pain — but said often, they can soften a guarded heart."
  - q: "How do I use heart chakra affirmations?"
    a: "Place a hand on the center of your chest, take a slow breath, and say one line as you exhale. Try them during meditation, journaling, or a quiet morning moment. Pick the two or three that feel true today rather than reciting all of them. Saying one line like you mean it matters more than how many you get through."
  - q: "What if loving myself does not feel true yet?"
    a: "That is okay and very common. Reach for the laddered lines — ones that say I am learning to or I am open to instead of asserting a feeling you do not have yet. You do not have to believe an affirmation perfectly for it to start softening your self-talk over time."
