## FAQ: Wealth Affirmations Explained

### Why do wealth affirmations work?

They reshape your inner dialogue. Repeating positive statements over time can rewire your subconscious, making room for healthier financial habits and decisions.

> “Whether you think you can, or you think you can’t—you’re right.” – Henry Ford

### Can affirmations really change my financial situation?

Not alone. But they create the belief that fuels action. Affirmations set the emotional tone that supports smart financial behavior—like saving, learning, and investing.

### How often should I say affirmations?

Start with once a day. Morning or night works well. Say them slowly, with feeling. One powerful line repeated daily can spark major change.

### How do I write my own wealth affirmations?

Flip your fears. If you fear debt, affirm “I am learning to manage money with clarity.” Keep it positive, present-tense, and emotionally true.
