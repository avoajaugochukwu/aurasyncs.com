## ❓ FAQ: Sweary Quotes & Daily Life

### Why do quotes resonate so deeply?

Because they name what we’re feeling—sometimes before we even know how to say it. Like a flashlight in emotional fog, they remind us we're not alone.

### Can quotes really change your mindset?

Yes. Repeat the right ones often enough, and they rewire the narrative. Changing “I can’t” to “I f*cking will” is more powerful than it sounds.

### What’s the best way to use these quotes?

Post them where you’ll see them—mirrors, desktops, lock screens, notebooks. Let them interrupt your self-doubt. Let them echo.

### How can I find my own favorite quotes?

Pay attention to what makes you feel something. If a line punches you in the chest, it belongs to you. Save it. Repeat it. Use it.
