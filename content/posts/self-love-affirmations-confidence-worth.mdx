faq:
  - q: "What are self-love affirmations?"
    a: "Self-love affirmations are short, positive statements you repeat to reinforce your worth and quiet negative self-talk — for example, ‘I am worthy of love and respect.’ Repeated consistently, they help reframe limiting beliefs and build confidence and self-acceptance."
  - q: "How long does it take for self-love affirmations to work?"
    a: "Most people notice a shift in mindset within a few weeks of daily practice, but it varies. Affirmations work through repetition, so consistency matters more than intensity — a few minutes every day beats an occasional long session."
  - q: "How do I use self-love affirmations effectively?"
    a: "Say them daily in the present tense (‘I love myself,’ not ‘I will’), believe in what you say (soften with ‘I am learning to…’ if needed), picture yourself living the words, and pair them with mirror work, journaling, or moments before bed."
  - q: "Do self-love affirmations actually have scientific backing?"
    a: "Yes. Research in Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience found self-affirmation improves problem-solving under stress, and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy uses affirmations to reframe negative thoughts. Studies link positive affirmations to lower stress and higher self-esteem."
