faq:
  - q: "Why do “I Am” affirmations resonate so deeply?"
    a: "They tie your sense of self to God's promises rather than your performance. Repeating a line like “I am loved” slowly reshapes how you see yourself, the way Romans 12:2 describes the mind being renewed."
  - q: "Can affirmations truly change my mindset spiritually?"
    a: "Yes. They work like spiritual muscle memory. Repeating “I am a child of God” moves a truth from something you know in your head to something you live. Over time your thoughts and actions begin to line up with what you declare."
  - q: "How can I use these affirmations daily?"
    a: "Speak one aloud each morning, write it in a journal, post it where you will see it, or meditate on a single line at a time. Small and steady beats long and rare."
  - q: "Is it okay to personalize biblical affirmations?"
    a: "Absolutely. Personalizing makes them more relatable, such as “I am strong through Christ as I face today.” Just keep the original meaning of the verse intact."
