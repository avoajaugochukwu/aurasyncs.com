## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Why do health affirmations work?**

Our thoughts shape how we experience our bodies and lives. Repeating health affirmations creates mental patterns that help us focus on healing, rather than fear.

> “What you focus on expands.”

**Q2: How do I use health affirmations daily?**

Keep it simple. Choose one affirmation and repeat it during a routine: brushing your teeth, walking, journaling. Consistency matters more than perfection.

> “Affirmations are like seeds. They need light, water, and time to grow.”

**Q3: Can affirmations help with stress, illness, or anxiety?**

Yes. While affirmations aren’t a cure, they can reduce stress, reframe negative thinking, and help you stay emotionally grounded during recovery or hardship.

**Q4: What if I don’t believe the affirmations yet?**

That’s okay. You’re not pretending—you’re practicing. Speak them with willingness, and belief may follow.

> “Say it until your soul starts to listen.”
