## ❓ FAQ: Using Weight Loss Affirmations

**Q: Why do affirmations matter in weight loss?**

They help rewire your internal dialogue. When you believe in your ability to grow, you make choices that align with your goals.

**Q: Can affirmations really shift my mindset?**

Yes—but like any habit, repetition matters. Think of affirmations as mental reps for a stronger mindset.

**Q: How can I use them daily?**

Pair them with routines—say one before meals, after workouts, or while brushing your teeth.

**Q: How do I write my own?**

Start with “I am” or “I choose.” Keep it positive and present tense. Example: “I am creating a healthy life, one step at a time.”
