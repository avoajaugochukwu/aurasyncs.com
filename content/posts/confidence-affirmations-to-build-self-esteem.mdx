## ❓ FAQ: Confidence & Affirmations

**Why do quotes feel so powerful?**

They’re concise truths. A quote is a shortcut to a feeling you’ve always had—but couldn’t express.

**Can affirmations really change my mindset?**

Yes, but it’s not instant. Like exercise, you build strength through repetition. What you repeat, you become.

**What’s the best way to use affirmations daily?**

Speak them aloud. Journal about them. Set reminders. Whisper them before interviews or during walks.

**Where can I find quotes that resonate with me?**

Start a digital “quote garden.” Save lines from books, films, songs. Notice what stirs something in you.
